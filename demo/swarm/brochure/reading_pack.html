AI models may be capable of self-replicating without a datacenter

anthropic.com/research/self-replicating-ai

The report behind the session. Read what it actually claims, and note the honest caveat: the end-to-end test ran on emulators with no security, so it's an upper bound, not a worm in the wild.

CISA advisory AA26-097A: attacks on water-sector control systems

cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa26-097a

Real attackers kept the normal control logic running but overrode the safe limits and switched off alarms. That "everything looks valid" move is the heart of today's demo.

EPA: what happens when water pressure drops

epa.gov · loss of pressure

Why 20 psi is the line. Below it, contaminated groundwater can be pulled into the pipes and fire sprinklers lose the pressure they need.

Understanding the Mirai botnet

USENIX Security 2017

In 2016 a worm took over ~600,000 devices with no AI at all — just factory-default passwords. A reminder that the basics still matter most.

CopyCatch: spotting lockstep behaviour

Meta Research, WWW 2013

The idea behind the defence you saw: catch a group acting in sync, even when each member looks fine on its own. Facebook used it against fake-Like rings.

Constrained concealment attacks against anomaly detectors in ICS

arxiv.org/abs/1907.07487

For the ambitious: how an attacker can shape data to slip past a detector. Changing just 4 of 82 sensors dropped one detector's catch rate from 68% to 12%.
