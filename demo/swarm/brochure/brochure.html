AI Swarm Attacks: Defending an IoT Fleet

Michael raised AI swarm attacks at the last company meeting. Our SME, Chaitanya, is going to show you what one actually looks like.

A fleet of 80 water regulators. Watch which ones you'd notice.

Why this, now

In August 2026, attackers changed equipment settings and switched off the alarms at two Colorado water utilities. And a new report warns that small AI models are getting good enough to spread on their own.

Small models "may be months away from being capable enough to self-replicate on commercial IoT devices." Anthropic Frontier Red Team, October 2026

Chaitanya will run a simulated swarm attack on a fleet of Hydroficient regulators and walk through why these attacks are so hard to spot until you are already under one — and what a security engineer does about it.
